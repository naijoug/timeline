# Filter usability improvement — 2026-10-25

## Observed issues

Live own-cloud desktop and narrow-browser inspection found fragmented controls, non-removable active-condition text, manual-only year entry, and unexplained pan/zoom icons. Changing a period performed a page navigation that lost search and ordering. The primary reading layout is unchanged.

## Scope

- Keep category and explicitly labeled milestone-only choice near the timeline; group period, year presets, custom years and regional scope in the expandable panel.
- Show removable active-condition chips and a genuine clear-all action. Clearing retains reading order, not hidden search/date/category restrictions.
- Preserve other facets when changing periods; period-bound routes canonicalize to the topic root when changing their implied period.
- Preserve full filter queries on directory and record-return links. Custom years remain explicit-apply; other controls act immediately.
- Provide clear result feedback, accessible validation, touch-sized controls, and close/Escape focus return.

## Verification boundary

Unit and production-hydration regression checks cover state serialization, period transitions, clearing, presets, invalid dates, and focus. Existing dual-base-path build/link/performance checks remain required. Actual responsive cloud-browser interaction is checked separately after publication; this is not a real iPhone or Safari test. No content data, dependencies, account settings or major layout redesign are included.
