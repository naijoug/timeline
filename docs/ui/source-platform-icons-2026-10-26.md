# Platform identity and theme icons

Source links identify their destination independently of the source author. Compact publisher-only links now show the destination icon, with a unique document title, publisher and new-window hint in their accessible name and native hover title (keyboard focus exposes the accessible link name). Full bibliographic lists retain title, author/publisher, date, version and locator. Numbered citations retain their numbers. Unknown sites use a globe and their parsed hostname, not a guessed brand.

Shared rendering covers AI comparison panels, legacy inline cards, all event references, entity/author/language/object pages, relationship citations, period sources, recommended reading and the about-page catalog. Local assets only: no favicon service, remote image tracking, hydrated icon islands or package dependency changes.

Brand artwork: Hugging Face official https://huggingface.co/front/assets/huggingface_logo-noborder.svg; GitHub, YouTube, Bilibili, arXiv and Wikipedia paths from Simple Icons via https://cdn.simpleicons.org/ (CC0 icon distribution; brands retain their trademark rights). Used as destination identifiers, not endorsement. Lucide (existing dependency, ISC) supplies generic globe and four distinct theme metaphors: BrainCircuit, CodeXml, Landmark and Feather. The sidebar open/close control keeps its action icon.

Tests cover parsed-host mapping, lookalike domains, URL credentials, malformed/protocol URLs, unique source names and distinct theme icons. Publication requires full build, link, performance, hydration, reading and actual desktop/narrow browser checks. No content dates or source URLs changed.
